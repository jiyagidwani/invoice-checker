    What this does and does not do. Fixed rules decide every verdict. This hosted copy has no AI and runs the rules only. In the Claude version, AI reads an invoice photo into the form and does nothing else. It cannot tell you whether a supplier is real or whether an invoice is genuine, and it does not check cess, e-invoicing or place of supply beyond the two GSTIN states.
